They say to let it go.

As though pain were a bird,
resting briefly,
before choosing another shoulder.

As though memory
were something the hands
could simply unclench.

ㅤ
The funeral ends quietly.

Flowers bow beneath rain.

Voices become distant.

The last pair of footsteps
leaves the cemetery,

and the earth is expected
to finish the sentence.

ㅤ
It never does.

ㅤ
The body goes first.

Then the name.

Then the photographs
fade into unfamiliar faces.

Time performs its work
with remarkable precision.

Everything softens.

Everything disappears.

Everything,

except one thing.

ㅤ
The last unanswered wound.

Still awake.

Still lying beneath
all that careful silence.

ㅤ
Dirt has never known
the difference

between flesh

and what flesh carried.

ㅤ
Roots pass through ribs.

Rain enters the coffin.

Wood forgets
it was once a tree.

Yet somewhere beneath it all,

one sentence
refuses to rot.

ㅤ
You knew.

ㅤ
Not louder.

Not softer.

Only unchanged.

ㅤ
People mistake quiet
for peace.

Cemeteries encourage that illusion.

Grass grows.

Stone sinks.

Seasons rehearse themselves
until grief begins
to resemble weather.

ㅤ
Nothing looks unfinished.

Everything is.

ㅤ
Forgiveness has always been spoken of
like clean water.

Necessary.

Pure.

Given freely.

No one speaks
of mouths
that learned
the taste of rust instead.

ㅤ
Some injuries
do not ask
to become lessons.

They ask
to remain true.

ㅤ
There are graves
visited every year,

not because the dead
are expected to answer,

but because forgetting

would feel like
helping them disappear.

Some wounds
deserve the same loyalty.

ㅤ
Years collapse into one another.

Rain smooths the headstone.

The letters lose their edges.

The flowers stop arriving.

The silence grows practiced.

Still,

beneath all of it,

the wound remains

with the patience
of buried iron.

ㅤ
There is no haunting.

No revenge.

No miracle waiting
beneath the soil.

Only the unbearable permanence

of something

that refused

to become smaller

for the comfort

of everyone else.
